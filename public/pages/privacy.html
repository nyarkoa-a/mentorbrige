Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use MentorBridge, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash), role (student or mentor), and any profile details you choose to provide, such as your programme, skills, career goals, biography, and professional history.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, search queries, filter selections, and time spent on the Platform. This is collected automatically via server logs and analytics tools.
  • Communications: Messages sent through the mentorship request system and any correspondence with our support team.
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the Platform, including powering the AI mentor matching algorithm using your profile data.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Send you notifications about mentorship requests, session reminders, and platform updates (you can opt out in Settings).
  • Personalise your experience — for example, showing recommended mentors based on your career field and interests.
  • Respond to your support requests and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Conduct aggregated, anonymised analytics to understand how the Platform is used and to identify areas for improvement.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption of data in transit (TLS), hashed password storage, and access controls that limit who can view user data internally.

Despite our efforts, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorised access to your account.

4. Cookies

MentorBridge uses the following types of cookies and local storage:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the Platform to function — for example, keeping you logged in during a session.
  • Preference storage: We store your theme preference (dark/light mode) in your browser's localStorage. This does not leave your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymised analytics tools to understand usage patterns. These do not identify you personally.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the Platform may not function correctly as a result.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your account and associated personal data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your data, such as analytics.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@mentorbridge.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services

MentorBridge may integrate with third-party services for authentication (Firebase), analytics, or communication. These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them. We only share the minimum data required for these services to function.

7. Contact for Privacy Concerns

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact our Data Privacy Officer at:

Email: privacy@mentorbridge.com
Address: MentorBridge, Accra, Ghana
