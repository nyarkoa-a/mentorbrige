Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you sign up.

Is MentorBridge really free for students?
Yes. Every feature available to mentees — matching, sessions, messaging, and the Pathfinder Certificate — is free for University of Ghana students.
How are mentors verified?
Mentors go through an identity check and a review of their professional background before they can appear in the directory or be matched with a mentee.
How does the matching process work?
After sign-up, you'll share your field of interest, goals, and availability. Our matching system suggests mentors whose experience fits, and you confirm the pairing before your first session.
How long is the 5-stage journey?
Most mentees complete all five stages — CV Writing, Professional Development, Industry Exposure, Career Preparation, and Industry Certification — over one academic semester, at a pace agreed with their mentor.
Can I change mentors if it's not a good fit?
Yes. You can request a new match at any time from your dashboard, and it won't affect your progress through the 5-stage journey.
What do I need to become a mentor?
A few years of industry experience and an hour or two a month. Apply from the "Become a Mentor" button — our team reviews applications within a week.
